Research

Human-Robot Interaction, Policy Steering, and Agentic Robotics. All about human.

RoboPrompt: Intuitive Robot Policy Steering with Sparse Human Input

Yanwen Zou, Chenyang Shi, Guoxuan Xu, Wenye Yu, Wendi Chen, Ye Pan, Cewu Lu†, Chuan Wen†

arXiv, 2026

Project page arXiv

RoboPrompt lets users steer diffusion and flow-matching robot policies with sparse target points, drawn traces, and coarse directional guidance. A reusable module turns human intent into action drafts that the base policy refines, enabling intuitive failure recovery and policy improvement through steered rollouts.

RoboRSI: Stable, Efficient, and Reusable Robot Self-Evolution in Complex Real-World Environments

Noematrix Team

Research Blog, 2026

Blog Code

RoboRSI combines multi-agent collaboration with Top-down Skill Refinement to develop reusable robot skills. It coordinates planning, execution, diagnosis, and local skill updates, enabling stable self-improvement for long-horizon mobile manipulation with human guidance and safety supervision.

Noe-0 Research Preview: Breaking Through the Dexterity Bottleneck with End-to-End Non-Embodied Data

Noematrix Team

Research Blog, 2026

Blog

Noe-0 co-designs data, models, and infrastructure for embodied foundation models. Non-embodied data in both pre-training and post-training preserves natural motion for dexterous manipulation, while joint action learning and implicit world modeling improve transfer across embodiments, objects, and scenes.

ActiveGlasses: Learning Manipulation with Active Vision from Ego-centric Human Demonstration

Yanwen Zou*, Chenyang Shi*, Wenye Yu, Han Xue, Jun Lv, Ye Pan, Chuan Wen†, Cewu Lu†

Project page arXiv

ActiveGlasses is a system for learning robot manipulation from ego-centric human demonstrations with active vision. To enable zero-transfer, we extract object trajectories from demonstrations and use an object-centric point-cloud policy to jointly predict manipulation and head movement.

Evo-1: Lightweight Vision-Language-Action Model with Preserved Semantic Alignment

Tao Lin, Yilei Zhong, Yuxin Du, Jingjing Zhang, Jiting Liu, Yinxinyu Chen, Encheng Gu, Ziyan Liu, Hongyi Cai, Yanwen Zou, Lixing Zou, Zhaoye Zhou, Gen Li, Bo Zhao†

CVPR 2026

Project page arXiv

Evo-1 builds on a native multimodal Vision-Language model (VLM), incorporating a novel cross-modulated diffusion transformer along with an optimized integration module, together forming an effective architecture. With only 0.77 billion parameters, Evo-1 achieves SOTA results on the Meta-World and RoboTwin suite.

U-ARM: Ultra low-cost general teleoperation interface for robot manipulation

Yanwen Zou, Zhaoye Zhou, Chenyang Shi, Zewei Ye, Junda Huang, Yan Ding, Bo Zhao

RSS 2026 RoboData Workshop

Project page arXiv

U-Arm is a low-cost, universal, leader-follower teleoperation system for any commercial robot arms through three hardware configurations. Designed for researchers, educators, and robotics enthusiasts, it provides a standardized interface for diverse robot platforms.

Evo-0: Vision-Language-Action Model with Implicit Spatial Understanding

Tao Lin, Gen Li, Yilei Zhong, Yanwen Zou, Yuxin Du, Jiting Liu, Encheng Gu, Bo Zhao†

Project page arXiv

Evo-0 introduces a plug-and-play module that implicitly injects 3D geometry features into VLA models by leveraging an off-the-shelf visual geometry foundation model.
